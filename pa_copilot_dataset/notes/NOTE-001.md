# Office visit note (NOTE-001)

## HPI
54-year-old male with low back pain beginning 2026-05-18 after lifting. Pain 7/10 radiating to the left lateral leg. Denies bowel or bladder changes, fever, or weight loss.

## Exam
Mild lumbar paraspinal tenderness. Straight leg raise positive on the left at 50 degrees. Strength 5/5 bilaterally.

## Assessment
Left lumbar radiculopathy, probable L5. No red flags.

## Plan
Start naproxen 500 mg BID. Refer to physical therapy 2x/week for 6 weeks. Home exercise program provided. Reassess in 6-8 weeks.
