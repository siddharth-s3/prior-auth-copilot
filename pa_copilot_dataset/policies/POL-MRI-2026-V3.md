# Lumbar Spine MRI (POL-MRI-2026-V3)
Version 3 | Effective 2026-04-01 to until superseded | Plans: PPO-GOLD, HMO-SILVER, MA-PLUS

## 1. Scope and applicability
1.1 This policy applies to MRI of the lumbar spine (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158) for adult members.
1.2 Applicable plans: PPO-GOLD, HMO-SILVER, MA-PLUS.
1.3 Governing date. The policy version in effect on the date the authorization request is received governs the review, regardless of the scheduled service date.
1.4 Precedence. Where a plan-specific lumbar spine MRI policy is in effect for the member's plan, the plan-specific policy governs.

## 2. Medical necessity criteria
All of the following must be met unless the red flag pathway (Section 3) or an exception (Section 5) applies.
- **C-01** Low back pain has persisted for at least 6 weeks (42 days), with the onset date documented.
- **C-02** At least 6 weeks of conservative therapy (supervised physical therapy, a documented home exercise program, and/or NSAIDs or analgesics). Therapy type, start date, and total duration MUST be documented.
- **C-03** A clinical examination (musculoskeletal and neurological) by the treating clinician is documented within 30 days before the request date.
- **C-04** No lumbar spine MRI or CT was performed in the prior 12 months, unless a new or progressive neurological deficit has been documented since that imaging.

## 3. Red flag pathway
3.1 RF-01. Any of: new or progressive motor deficit, suspected cauda equina syndrome, suspected spinal infection, or suspected malignancy. When RF-01 is documented, C-01 and C-02 are waived. C-03 still applies.

## 4. Exclusions
4.1 EX-01. Routine imaging for non-specific low back pain without red flags before the minimum symptom and treatment durations are met.
4.2 EX-02. Imaging requested solely at patient request without clinical indication.

## 5. Exceptions
5.1 EXC-001 Post-surgical. Lumbar spine surgery within the prior 12 months with new or worsening symptoms waives C-01 and C-02. Valid 2026-04-01 through 2026-12-31.
5.2 EXC-002 Oncology history. A documented history of malignancy within the prior 5 years with new back pain waives C-01 and C-02. Valid from 2026-04-01.

## 6. Rationale
6.1 MRI is indicated when persistent low back pain has not responded to conservative treatment, or when red flags are present.
